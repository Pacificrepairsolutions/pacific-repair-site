    FAQ

    Questions people ask

    Don’t see yours? Call 916-235-0084 and a real person will answer.

    Do you offer same-day service?

    In most cases, yes. Same-day appointments depend on the schedule and where you are in the Sacramento area. Calling before noon gives you the best chance.

    How much does a repair cost?

    It depends on the problem, the part and the model. If we do the repair, the diagnostic is free — and you approve a fixed price before any work starts.

    Do you repair and install garbage disposals?

    Yes. We fix jammed, humming, leaking and dead disposals — InSinkErator, Waste King, Moen and others — and install new ones the same day.

    Do you work on gas and electric appliances?

    Yes. We repair gas and electric dryers, ovens, ranges and cooktops.

    Can you fix my AC and furnace too?

    Yes. We repair and tune up central AC, heat pumps, furnaces, ductless mini-splits and thermostats. A spring tune-up helps avoid a breakdown on the hottest day of summer.

    What electrical work do you do?

    Outlets and switches, lighting and ceiling fans, dedicated appliance circuits, EV charger installs, panel upgrades and breakers that keep tripping.

    Do you repair luxury brands like Sub-Zero, Viking and Miele?

    Yes. We work on built-in and high-end appliances including Sub-Zero, Wolf, Viking, Thermador, Miele, Dacor, Monogram, GE Profile and JennAir, plus every major brand.

    How long does a repair take?

    Most repairs take 1–2 hours. If a part has to be ordered, we book a quick return visit as soon as it arrives.
